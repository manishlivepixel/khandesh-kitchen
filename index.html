Our Story · आमची गोष्ट

From the heart of खान्देश, to your plate in Badlapur

Khandesh Kitchen brings the legendary cuisine of North Maharashtra to Katrap, Badlapur. Our recipes come straight from Khandeshi households — where food is loud with flavour, generous with spice, and always made like it's for family.

Every morning our kitchen grinds fresh masalas — the iconic खान्देशी तेच, the deep-red रस्सा, and slow-cooked curries that taste of home. Pair them with soft भाकरी straight off the tawa, and you'll understand why Khandeshi food has a fan following across Maharashtra.

  • Authentic Khandeshi RecipesTraditional home-style cooking, no shortcuts
  • Freshly Ground SpicesMasalas prepared in-house every day
  • Veg & Non-Veg DelicaciesFrom पिठले–भाकरी to मटण रस्सा
  • Family RestaurantClean, comfortable & warm hospitality
